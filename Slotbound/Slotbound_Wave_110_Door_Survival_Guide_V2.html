When should General stop receiving levels?

My recommended replacement window is around General LV 60–100, when a viable upgrade is available. This is a planning checkpoint, not a tested optimal breakpoint, a guaranteed wave-110 strategy, or a forced cap. Switch earlier if the replacement is already ready. General’s level and the current wave are different numbers.

General’s Absorb LVMy suggested decision
30First awakening and first review. Start preparing a suitable higher-rarity melee unit if one appears.
60Main replacement checkpoint. If a viable Rare or Legendary is ready, redirect most new fodder to it.
100Last awakening checkpoint. Do not keep chasing General levels simply because a Prism has not appeared.
Over 100Continue only as needed while he is your best available frontline. Do not weaken a live run by freezing its only workable tank.
Already 200+Check the replacement preview carefully. Build the new unit’s readiness before removing General; lost transfer efficiency makes a late switch more delicate.

Readiness matters more than a number

  1. Promote the replacement: Rare three-star forms start at LV 18; Legendary at LV 20.
  2. Use LV 30 as a first readiness check and awakening opportunity, not proof it can replace a much higher-level tank.
  3. Install sustain and useful damage or control effects. Check traits, HP, attack speed and your actual failure mode.
  4. If army space permits, keep General during the transition and direct surplus fodder into the candidate.
  5. Inspect the absorption preview before committing General. Check the resulting form, level and imprint choices.
  6. Once the replacement can anchor the army, make it the main investment. A continuing main carry should not stop at 30.

For an existing LV 200 General, do not assume a newly promoted LV 18–30 Rare is instantly safer. Level growth, skills, traits and installed imprints all affect the comparison. There is no universal minimum replacement level. See the absorption and individual-unit references in the dropdown.

02 / SPEND LEVELS WITH PURPOSE

Where the healer pauses

Pause a safe, functioning High Priest at 30 first. Its heal is percentage-based; the description does not say caster ATK increases the healing percentage. It cannot heal itself. Further levels can improve survival and unlock additional awakenings.

60

Selective investment

100

Later support upgrade

Third awakening. Optional investment when it solves a specific problem.

Awakenings to consider

  • Focused Heal for concentrated frontline healing.
  • Shared Ward against area damage.
  • Spell Ward against skill damage.

Choose from the offered options and match their positioning conditions.

Two different kinds of level

Absorb LV grows the unit. Imprint I–V upgrades an installed effect. Raising a unit to LV 30 does not automatically give its imprints level V.

Unlock Dormant Power for awakenings. Unit stat growth continues after 30 at a reduced rate.

The preparation loop

  1. Retain expendable fodder of the class you want.
  2. Absorb it before the next first spin.
  3. Use Bloodline Seal’s class symbols with Black Note’s Joker.
  4. Inspect important imprint donors before mass absorption.
  5. Feed spare units mainly into the carry.
  6. Keep suitable fodder for the next preparation phase.

This sequence is inferred from the core descriptions. Bloodline Seal follows the sacrificed unit’s class, not the receiving carry’s class. X2 spends extra gold; use it when affordable.

06 / TURN THE BUILD INTO A RUN

Position, choose, prepare

Positioning

  • Carry ahead of the support army.
  • High Priests protected and within healing range.
  • Undine’s waves passing through allies.
  • Ranged units spread when area damage is the problem.
  • Adjacency requirements on awakenings respected.

Investment order

  1. Functional frontline and first High Priest.
  2. Carry promotion and Lifesteal.
  3. Basic support Poison and crowd control.
  4. Carry awakenings at 30, 60 and 100.
  5. Established supports toward 30.
  6. More carry levels; selective support upgrades.

Use the door survival guide above for conditional first picks, fallback choices and reward tiebreaks.

Prepare before wave 100. The Gatekeeper is a strengthened final boss. Overtime after 60 seconds escalates damage and reduces healing, so a team that survives but clears too slowly can still fail.

Before the next boss

There is no verified exact carry level that guarantees wave 110. Fix the immediate failure before following an investment order mechanically.

What I would change on your next attempt

  1. Keep support units functional, promoted and safe. Use LV 30 as the first pause for established 3-star supports.
  2. Improve their important Poison and control imprints. This is separate from chasing high unit levels.
  3. Use General as a bridge. Use the four non-Prism options above as your replacement shortlist, matching the unit to the current weakness.
  4. Give most surplus fodder to the current carry; selectively buy a support awakening only when it solves a clear problem.
  5. Before merging General into an upgrade, verify the preview. Levels do not transfer one-for-one.
  6. Before wave 65, check damage, control and door modifiers. More healing alone does not fix slow kills.
Clarifying the earlier guide: support LV 60 and 100 are optional awakening purchases. They should not be routine targets for every support while a struggling General is doing almost all the work.

When none of the doors looks good

For your healer-dependent, Poison-supported melee plan, Ash and Venom usually deserve extra scrutiny. If every offer is dangerous, select the one that least worsens the problem you actually have. A reward that immediately fixes that problem can break a close tie. [D2–D3]

Frontline collapses early

Start with Frost or Deep if clear speed allows it. Be wary of Blood, Sand and Venom. Shadow is helpful only if its melee benefit fits your damage mix.

Frontline holds, fights drag on

Move Frost down. Shadow fits a melee-led damage race. Blood or Sand become candidates only with a comfortable survival margin; lower HP is not worth an immediate frontline collapse.
